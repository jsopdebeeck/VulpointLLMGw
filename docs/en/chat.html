User guide

Using your organisation's AI chat

The chat gives you access to the AI models your organisation allows. Your messages go through a gateway that protects sensitive data before it leaves the network.

Version 1.5.5Address /chat on your organisation's gatewayLanguages English, French, DutchScreenshots French interface

1What the chat is for

The chat works like consumer AI assistants: you type a question and the AI answers. The difference is that everything goes through your organisation's LLMGW gateway:

  • you only use the models allowed for your profile;
  • names, emails, file numbers, INAMI numbers and other sensitive data are replaced by tokens before being sent to the AI, then put back in clear text in the answer you read;
  • some files may be replaced or refused, depending on your profile's rules.

You do not need to do anything special: protection is automatic. This guide explains what you see on screen and how to respond to a message from the gateway.

2Signing in

Open the gateway's /chat address (your IT department will give it to you). There are two ways to sign in:

  • With your work account: the “Sign in with Microsoft” or “Sign in with Google” button. This is the recommended method; the session lasts 12 hours by default.
  • With a personal key (starting with gw-), provided by the administrator. It stays in the browser tab and disappears when you close it.

The list at the bottom of the card lets you choose the language right from this screen.

Requesting access

If the administrator has enabled it, the “Request access” link appears below the key field. Enter your name, your work email address and, if you wish, the reason for your request.

  1. You receive an email: open the link it contains, then click “Confirm”. The link is valid for 24 hours and can only be used once.
  2. If your address belongs to a domain recognised by the organisation, your access is opened straight away and your key is shown on the page. Copy it immediately: it will not be shown again.
  3. Otherwise, your request is forwarded to the administrator. If they accept it, you receive an email with a link to show your key, once only. If there is no answer within two weeks, the request is refused.

Your key is never sent to you by email.

Your key is personal. Do not share it and do not paste it into another tool: everything done with it is attributed to you. If in doubt, ask the administrator for a new key.

4Asking a question

  1. Choose a model from the list in the header. Models are grouped by provider; your choice is remembered.
  2. Type your message. Enter sends it, Shift + Enter starts a new line.
  3. The answer appears as it is written. The “Stop” button interrupts an answer that is too long.
  4. To change the subject, click “New conversation”: the AI starts again without the previous context.

The AI keeps no memory between two conversations; within the same conversation, it receives the history of the exchange with each question.

5What the gateway does with your data

Before sending, the gateway finds sensitive data and replaces it with tokens. The AI works on the tokens; the gateway puts the real values back in the answer you read.

1 · You type
“Draft a reply for Ms Claire Dubois (claire.dubois@acme.be), file DOS-2025-00042.”
2 · Gateway
Finds a person, an email, a file number.
3 · The AI receives
“… for Ms «PERSON_1» («EMAIL_1»), file «DOSSIER_1».”
4 · Gateway
Puts the real values back in the answer.
5 · You read
“We are getting back to you regarding Claire Dubois…”

The badge ⇄ “3 value(s) pseudonymised: DOSSIER ×1, EMAIL ×1, PERSON ×1” under the message shows how many values were protected, and of which types.

Depending on your profile, some data may be forbidden (for example a national register number or an IBAN in a “health” profile): the question is then refused, nothing is sent, and a message tells you so. Rephrase it without that data.

6Attaching documents and images

Click “Attach” or drag files into the input area. Accepted formats: text (txt, md, csv, json, xml, html, log, yaml), PDF, Word (docx) and images. The text of documents is extracted in your browser (up to 300,000 characters; beyond that the document is truncated), then goes through the gateway just like your messages.

If a PDF uses a badly encoded font, some letters are extracted as symbols (“J@an S©basti@”): the chat then shows “⚠ damaged extracted text” next to the document. The gateway still masks the names concerned, but the AI will receive poor-quality text; use another version of the document (Word, re-exported PDF) or copy the useful passage into your message.

Your profile decides what happens to each type of file:

Profile ruleWhat you seeWhat the AI receives
senta normal answerthe document or image
white squarea normal answer; the AI often says it cannot see the contenta white square instead of the image, or “[contenu remplacé par le gateway]” (content replaced by the gateway) instead of the document's text
blockedthe message “file forbidden by your profile: file name”nothing: the question is not sent

The reminder at the bottom of the screen shows your profile's rule for images (for example “images replaced by a white square”).

Files are logged. For most rules, the gateway records the file's name, size and fingerprint for 30 days (never its content). Some blocked files also trigger an alert to the security officer. Only attach what the AI needs.

7Conversation history

  • Your 10 most recent conversations are shown in the left panel, the newest at the top. They are kept in your browser only, not on the server.
  • Click a conversation to reopen it, or use the ⋯ menu: “Show” or “Delete”.
  • Images are not kept (too large): a reopened conversation only shows their names.
  • On another computer or another browser, the history is empty.

On a shared computer, delete your conversations and sign out at the end of your session.

8Language and theme

  • Language: the list in the header switches the interface to English, French or Dutch. You can write to the AI in any language you like, whatever the interface language.
  • Theme: the ◐ button switches between light and dark.
  • Sign out: closes the SSO session or forgets the key stored in the tab.

10Good practice

  • Give context: role, goal, expected format (“in 5 points”, “formal tone”). The answers are better.
  • Keep personal data to the strict minimum, even though the gateway protects it: automatic detection is not infallible, especially for a single name it has never seen.
  • Check the answers: the AI can make mistakes or invent things. Always reread before passing a text on.
  • If an answer contains a token in quotation marks («PERSON_1»), the AI altered it and the gateway could not restore it: replace it by hand or ask the question again.
  • For a new subject, open a new conversation: the AI is more accurate and each question costs fewer tokens.

11Frequently asked questions

Does the AI provider see my data?

It sees your text, except for the sensitive data detected and replaced by tokens. The mapping between tokens and values never leaves the gateway.

Are my conversations recorded?

The history is stored in your browser. The gateway records statistics (volumes, durations, number of protected values) without the content, plus the file log (name, size, fingerprint). A temporary troubleshooting mode can be enabled by the administrator; it is flagged in the administration dashboard.

Why does a model not appear in the list?

Only the models allowed for your profile and actually reachable are offered.

Can I use another tool (ChatGPT, Claude…) directly?

No: direct access to providers is blocked by the organisation's AI policy. Compatible tools (Claude Code, agents, scripts) connect to the gateway with an application key; see the administration documentation.
